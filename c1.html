Asli problem

Roz ka wahi chakkar, aur poora din nikal jaata hai

Ye chaar cheezein har institute ka sabse zyada time khaati hain — aur inhi mein sabse zyada admission leak hote hain.

Enquiry chhoot jaati hai

Raat 9 baje aayi call ya WhatsApp ka jawab agle din milta hai — tab tak parent doosre institute ja chuka hota hai.

Fee ke liye baar-baar call

Same sawaal 50 baar

“Timing kya hai? Fees kitni? Demo class milegi?” — staff din bhar yahi type karta rehta hai.

Record register mein

Kaunsa follow-up baaki hai, kiski fee pending hai — sab copy ya yaaddaasht mein. Numbers dikhte hi nahi.

Solution

Ek system, chaar kaam — bilkul apne aap

Aapke wahi WhatsApp number par chalega. Naya app, naya number ya nayi training — kuch bhi zaroori nahi.

📥

1. Admission enquiry auto-capture

Enquiry kahin se bhi aaye — WhatsApp, missed call ya Instagram — system turant reply karta hai, naam/class/subject poochta hai aur lead ko list mein save kar deta hai. Raat 2 baje bhi.

💰

2. Fee reminder automation

Due date se 3 din pehle polite reminder, due date par yaad-dilawa, late hone par gentle follow-up. Payment aate hi receipt WhatsApp par, aur naam list se apne aap hat jaata hai.

💬

3. Parent query auto-answer

Timing, fees, syllabus, holiday, test date, result — common sawaalon ke ready jawab. Jo sawaal system na samjhe, wo turant staff ko forward ho jaata hai, taaki koi parent ignore mehsoos na kare.

📊

4. Owner dashboard + daily report

Roz raat 9 baje aapke phone par summary: kitni enquiry aayi, kitni convert hui, kitni fee pending hai. Poore institute ka haal ek nazar mein, kisi se poochhe bina.

Ek enquiry ka poora safar — 5 step

Isme aapke staff ko sirf ek jagah kaam karna hai: step 4 ki call.

1

Parent message karta hai

Aapke usi WhatsApp number par, ya poster/Google ke link se. Time koi bhi ho — raat ya Sunday.

2

System 5 second mein reply karta hai

Institute ke naam se professional greeting, batch options, fees aur demo class ka offer — sab ek ready message mein.

3

Details apne aap save

Naam, class, subject, number aur source ek clean list mein chala jaata hai — na register, na bhoolne ka darr.

4

Staff ko ready lead milta hai

Subah staff ke paas taiyaar list hoti hai — sirf call karke close karna hai. Lead thanda pade to system khud 2 follow-up bhejta hai.

5

Admission ke baad fee cycle shuru

Student add hote hi uski fee date set ho jaati hai, aur har mahine reminder khud chalta rehta hai. Aapko kuch yaad rakhna hi nahi hai.

Sawaal-jawab

Aapke man ke sawaal, seedha jawab

Kya mera WhatsApp number badalna padega?
Nahi. Aapka wahi number, wahi chat. System usi ke upar kaam karta hai — parents ko farak sirf itna dikhega ki reply turant aata hai.
Staff ko technical knowledge chahiye?
Bilkul nahi. Agar staff WhatsApp chala leta hai to ye panel bhi chala lega. 10 minute ki training kaafi hai, aur main screen share par samjha bhi dunga.
Parents ko robot jaisa lagega to?
Messages aapki apni bhasha aur tone mein likhe jaate hain — “Namaste, Tutor Point mein aapka swagat hai…”. Aur jaise hi baat serious hoti hai, chat turant staff ko chali jaati hai. Ye staff ko replace nahi karta, unka bojh halka karta hai.
Purana student data kaise aayega?
Register ho ya Excel — jo bhi aap use karte hain, mujhe de dijiye. Main khud import kar dunga, aapko ek entry type nahi karni padegi.
Data safe rahega?
Ji haan. Student aur parent details sirf aapke panel mein rehti hain, kisi ke saath share nahi hoti. Aap kabhi bhi poora data export ya delete karwa sakte hain.
Aage jaake band karna ho to?
Koi lock-in nahi. Jab chaahein band karwa dijiye — aapka number, chats aur data sab aapke paas hi rahega.
