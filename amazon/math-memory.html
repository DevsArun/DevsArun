Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

🛡️ The short version

This app collects zero data. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no internet. Everything stays on your device.

📵 100% Offline 🚫 No Ads 🔒 No Tracking 👶 COPPA Safe
👋

1. Who we are

Math Memory Planet (“the App”) is a memory and math game for children ages 3–12, published by ITS School (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the App handles — which is, happily, almost nothing.

🔍

2. Information we collect

None. Zero. Nothing. 🎉

  • ❌ No personal information
  • ❌ No location data
  • ❌ No camera / mic / contacts
  • ❌ No advertising identifiers
  • ❌ No analytics or tracking
  • ❌ No cookies

The App requests zero Android permissions and works completely offline. It never connects to the internet.

📱

3. Data stored on your device only

To make the game work, the App saves a small amount of progress data locally on your device only:

  • ⭐ Level progress, stars, streaks, and trophies
  • 🏷️ The child’s chosen display name (shown on screen only, never transmitted)
  • 🎨 Language, sound, and theme preferences

This data never leaves the device. Deleting the App, or using Settings → Reset progress, removes it permanently.

👶

4. Children’s privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

The App is designed for children and built to comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR rules for children. Because the App collects and transmits no data at all, no parental consent for data collection is required — there is simply nothing to consent to.

Parent-only areas (settings and progress statistics) are protected by a parental gate. 🔐

🚫

5. Ads and purchases

  • ❌ No advertisements of any kind
  • ❌ No in-app purchases
  • ❌ No links out to websites or social media from the child experience

You pay once when you buy the App. Everything inside is included. 🎁

🤝

6. Third parties

The App contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics services, and no ad networks. Your app store (e.g., Amazon Appstore) handles the purchase itself under its own privacy policy.

🔄

7. Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the App handles data, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date above before the change takes effect.

📧
