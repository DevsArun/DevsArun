Math Buddies

Privacy Policy

Simple, honest, and written for parents — no legal fog.

​

Last updated: August 10, 2026 · Applies to the Math Buddies app on the Amazon Appstore

​
The short version: Math Buddies collects no personal data at all. The app has no internet access, no ads, no analytics, no accounts, and no in-app purchases. Your child's progress never leaves the device.
​

🧒 1. Information we collect

None. Math Buddies does not collect, request, store, or share any personal information — no names, no email addresses, no phone numbers, no location, no device identifiers, no photos, no microphone or camera access, and no contacts.

​

🌐 2. Internet & network use

The app does not connect to the internet and does not request the internet permission. Every game, sound, and feature works completely offline, right out of the box.

​

📢 3. Ads, analytics & tracking

There is no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no third-party tracking of any kind. The app contains no third-party SDKs that collect data.

​

👤 4. Accounts & sign-in

No account, registration, or sign-in is required — for children or adults.

​

💾 5. Data stored on the device

To make the game fun, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • Stars, stickers, and trophies earned
  • The sticker scene your child decorates
  • Age group (3–4 or 5–6) chosen at first launch
  • Sound, music, and Buddy color settings

This data is never transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app removes it completely.

​

🛒 6. Purchases

Math Buddies is a one-time paid app. The purchase is handled entirely by the Amazon Appstore — we never see, process, or store any payment information. There are no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

​

🛡️ 7. Children's privacy (COPPA)

Math Buddies is designed for children ages 3–6. Because the app collects nothing and never connects to the internet, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by design. The grown-ups area is protected by a parental gate.

​

🔗 8. Data sharing & third parties

We share nothing because we have nothing. No data ever leaves the device, so there is nothing to sell, rent, or share with anyone.

​

✏️ 9. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date. The app's core promise will not change: no data collection, ever.

​

📬 10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email the developer at shyadav1298@gmail.com or use the developer contact link on the Amazon Appstore listing.
