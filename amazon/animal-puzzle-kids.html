We collect nothing

Animal Puzzle Kids is a fully offline children's game. It does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. No name, email, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or device identifiers are ever accessed.

📵

No internet, no ads, no tracking

The app has no internet permission, shows no advertising, uses no analytics, and contains no third-party SDKs that collect data. It works 100% offline, everywhere - even in airplane mode.

🧒

Children & COPPA

The app is designed for children and complies with COPPA: it collects no personal data from anyone, including children. The parents area is protected by a math-based parental gate.

💾

On-device only

All game progress (stars, stickers, streaks, language choice) is stored only on your device using local storage. It never leaves the device, and deleting the app deletes everything.
