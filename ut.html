01 PROJECT OVERVIEW

What exactly are we building?

A dedicated online place where your workshop information, learner enquiries and registrations come together.

Think of it as a digital front desk for your workshops: learners can find the right session and submit their details, while your team manages every request from one secure dashboard.

Clear workshop information

Give learners an easy way to understand the session, schedule, audience, format and fee before they decide.

One place for each request

Capture enquiries and registration details in a structured format, rather than relying only on individual conversations.

A more organised team

Help staff see what needs a reply, which registrations need review and which seats have been confirmed.

These are proposed improvements. Your existing process and actual requirements would be reviewed before the final scope is agreed.
02 HOW IT WORKS

From first visit to confirmed seat.

A straightforward journey for learners, with your academy staying in control of approvals and availability.

STEP 01

Discover a workshop

The learner opens the workshop page and sees what the session covers, who it is for, the date, fee and available format.

STEP 02

Enquire or register

If they have questions, they send an enquiry. If ready, they choose a session and submit a short registration form.

STEP 03

The team reviews

The request appears in the academy dashboard. Staff review details, answer questions and check session availability.

STEP 04

Confirm & prepare

Staff confirm eligible registrations. Learners see clear next steps, and the team has an organised participant list.

Registration statusRequest submittedAwaiting reviewSeat confirmed

Submitting a form is not the same as a confirmed seat. Enquiries remain separate from registrations. Confirmation rules would be agreed with your team.

03 THE TWO EXPERIENCES

Easy for learners.
Useful for your team.

The public website helps people take the next step. The private dashboard gives staff the tools to manage what happens afterwards.

Learner-facing website

Public access · No account proposed for version one

  • Browse available workshops

    View descriptions, learning objectives, intended audience, schedules, format, venue and fees.

  • Ask a question before joining

    A short enquiry form captures the learner’s question and contact details for staff follow-up.

  • Register through a simple form

    Select a session and submit the agreed details, such as name, email and phone number.

  • Receive a reference and next steps

    See an on-screen acknowledgement with a unique reference and a clear “Awaiting review” status.

  • Use it comfortably on any device

    A responsive layout with readable content, clear buttons and accessible forms.

Academy management dashboard

Private access · Authorised staff only

  • Publish and update workshops

    Create or edit workshop details, dates, fees, capacity and whether registrations are open.

  • Track enquiries and registrations

    Find requests by name, workshop or reference; filter by status and open the full details.

  • Review requests and manage seats

    Approve registrations within capacity and record cancelled requests or completed follow-ups.

  • Prepare participant lists

    See confirmed learners for each session and export the required registration data as CSV.

  • Keep staff access controlled

    Secure sign-in and agreed permissions help protect learner information from public access.

04 PROPOSED PROJECT SCOPE

Start with what matters.

A focused first version is easier to understand, test and maintain. Optional integrations can be added only if they support your workflow.

Public website

Learner experience

A branded workshop listing, individual workshop details, enquiry and registration forms, validation and on-screen acknowledgement. Final content and required form fields are agreed during discovery.

Staff dashboard

Academy operations

Staff login, workshop and session management, capacity settings, enquiry tracking, registration review, booking-status updates, participant lists and CSV export.

Application & data

Behind the scenes

A backend and central database to store workshop information and learner requests. Server-side validation, authorised access and capacity checks would support a reliable registration process.

Launch & handover

Ready for your team

Responsive testing, end-to-end workflow checks, deployment to the agreed hosting environment, source-code handover and a short guide or walkthrough for academy staff.

Optional additions—not part of the proposed core scope

Online payment gateway • Automated email or WhatsApp confirmations • Reminder messages • Attendance tracking • Certificates • Advanced reporting • Existing-tool integrations

These require separate agreement on scope, costs and providers. Payment rules, refunds, message consent and provider approvals must be defined before integration.

Not assumed in version one: learner accounts, a mobile app, an LMS/video-course platform, a full CRM or a complete redesign of the academy’s existing website.

Simple on the outside.

07 COMMON QUESTIONS

A few things, made clear.

Does this replace our current website?

Not necessarily. The registration experience could become a section of your current website or a separate portal linked from it. The best option depends on your existing website, hosting access and preferred workflow.

Can learners still contact us directly?

Yes. This platform is intended to organise the workshop journey, not remove human support. Agreed contact options can remain available, and website enquiries can be captured in the staff dashboard for follow-up.

Are online payments and WhatsApp messages included?

Not in the proposed core scope. They can be added as optional integrations after payment rules, message consent, provider requirements and charges are agreed. A confirmed registration should not automatically be treated as proof of payment.

What information would you need from the academy?

Your logo and branding, workshop descriptions, schedules, fees, capacity rules, enquiry and confirmation process, required learner fields, staff access requirements and current website details. Access credentials would be shared through an agreed secure method, not added to this proposal.

Is this an actual demo of the application?

This is a project proposal explaining the solution, not a functional application demo. Once the scope is agreed, a working prototype or test version can be planned as a development milestone for review.
